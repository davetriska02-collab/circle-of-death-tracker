How to use
  1. Select your site, role, and session type, then start.
  2. Press Space (or tap the green button) when a system freezes.
  3. Press Space again when it recovers. Add an optional note.
  4. Hold End Session for 2 seconds, review, and submit.

Do not enter patient names or NHS numbers. Privacy notice.
